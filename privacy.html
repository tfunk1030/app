Privacy Policy

AICaddyPro - Your AI Golf Caddy

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

Overview

AICaddyPro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our golf shot calculator application.

Key Point: AICaddyPro is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data, store most information locally on your device, and never sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

Location Data

When you grant permission, we access your device's location to:

  • Fetch current weather conditions for your area
  • Provide accurate environmental adjustments for your shots

Your location is sent to our weather data provider (OpenWeatherMap) to retrieve conditions. We do not store your location history on our servers.

Local Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your club distances and preferences
  • App settings and configurations
  • Shot history and calculations

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the app:

  • Crash reports via Sentry (helps us fix bugs)
  • Anonymous usage statistics (features used, app performance)

This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Third-Party Services

AICaddyPro uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenWeatherMap - Provides weather data (receives your location when requested)
  • RevenueCat - Manages subscriptions and purchases
  • Sentry - Crash reporting and error tracking
  • Apple App Store - Handles payments and app distribution

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide accurate shot calculations based on weather conditions
  • Remember your club distances and preferences
  • Process subscription payments through Apple
  • Improve app stability and fix bugs
  • Analyze anonymous usage to improve features

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • All network communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Local data is stored using secure device storage
  • We do not maintain servers storing your personal data

Your Rights

You have control over your data:

  • Access: View your stored preferences within the app
  • Delete: Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Location: Revoke location permission anytime in device settings
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics in app settings (where available)

Children's Privacy

AICaddyPro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
