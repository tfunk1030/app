# Privacy Policy for AICaddyPro

**Last Updated:** January 6, 2025

## Overview

AICaddyPro ("we", "our", or "the app") is a golf assistance application that helps golfers calculate shot distances based on environmental conditions. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- **What:** GPS coordinates when the app is in use
- **Why:** To provide accurate local weather and environmental conditions for shot calculations
- **Storage:** Processed locally on your device; not transmitted to external servers

### Device Sensors
- **What:** Compass heading, accelerometer data
- **Why:** To determine wind direction relative to your shot
- **Storage:** Processed locally on your device; never stored or transmitted

### Club Settings
- **What:** Your golf club names and distances
- **Why:** To provide personalized shot recommendations
- **Storage:** Stored locally on your device using secure storage

### Subscription Information
- **What:** Purchase status, subscription tier
- **Why:** To provide access to premium features
- **Handled by:** RevenueCat (our subscription provider)
- **Their Privacy Policy:** https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
- Account registration data
- Usage analytics or tracking
- Advertising identifiers
- Social media connections

## Third-Party Services

### Weather Data
We use Open-Meteo and the National Weather Service API to fetch weather conditions. These services receive your approximate location (city-level) to provide weather data. No personally identifiable information is shared.

### RevenueCat
For subscription management, we use RevenueCat. They process subscription purchases through Apple's App Store. See their privacy policy for details on how they handle payment information.

## Data Security

- All club settings are stored locally using encrypted device storage
- No user data is transmitted to our servers
- Location data is used only while the app is active

## Your Rights

You can:
- Delete all local data by uninstalling the app
- Revoke location permissions in your device settings
- Manage subscriptions through Apple's App Store

## Children's Privacy

AICaddyPro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision.
