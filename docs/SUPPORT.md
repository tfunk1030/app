## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I calibrate my club distances?

1. Go to **Settings** > **Clubs**
2. Tap on any club to edit its distance
3. Enter your average carry distance for that club
4. The app will use these distances for shot recommendations

**Tip:** Use your actual carry distances, not total distances including roll.

### Why are my wind calculations different from expected?

The wind calculator accounts for:
- Wind speed and direction relative to your shot
- Air density (temperature, altitude, humidity)
- Ball flight physics with Magnus effect

Make sure your compass is calibrated by moving your phone in a figure-8 pattern.

### How do I use the compass?

1. Open the **Wind** tab
2. Point your phone in the direction you're hitting
3. Tap **Lock** to lock the direction
4. The app will calculate wind effects relative to your shot line

### Why does the app need my location?

Location is used to:
- Fetch accurate local weather conditions
- Calculate altitude adjustments for your shots
- Provide real-time environmental data

Your location is never stored or shared.

### How do I switch between Imperial and Metric units?

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Units**
3. Choose **Imperial** (yards, mph, °F) or **Metric** (meters, km/h, °C)

### How do I restore my subscription?

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Scroll to **Subscription**
3. Tap **Restore Purchases**

If you're still having issues, make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

## Subscription Management

To manage your subscription:
1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Select **AICaddyPro**

## Known Issues

### Compass Not Working
- Some protective cases can interfere with the compass
- Try calibrating by moving your phone in a figure-8 pattern
- Ensure you've granted motion sensor permissions

### Location Not Available
- Check that Location Services are enabled for AICaddyPro
- Try toggling Location permission off and on in Settings
